# Bigg Boss India — SEO & Content Keyword Map

## Main goal
Capture real search intent around current Bigg Boss developments while keeping every page useful, verifiable and genuinely updated. The site should win traffic by answering the user's exact question — especially **who appeared, what happened, who advanced, who was not selected and what happens next**.

## Content model for every verified participant
When evidence supports it, record:
1. Name
2. Appearance/pre-show episode
3. Round/stage
4. Task/challenge
5. Result: appeared / selected / on hold / not selected / house contestant
6. Why the decision happened, if reported
7. Next round or next status
8. Source and date checked

Never fill an unknown field with an inference. Do not turn an audition result into a house eviction.

## Editorial rules
1. Use one primary intent per page.
2. Put the main phrase naturally in title, H1, first paragraph and relevant internal link text.
3. Do not list or repeat keyword variants unnaturally.
4. Create focused pages only for real developments with enough verified information.
5. Clearly label confirmed, reported and rumoured information.
6. Use “Today” only when the page contains a genuinely current update; change the date when content changes.
7. Regional-language variants can be added naturally when the page has useful content for that language; do not create translated keyword-only pages.
8. Link every focused story back to its edition hub and to one or two genuinely related stories.
9. Prefer a complete verified answer over a long list of unverified names.
10. If a source does not provide a full list, explicitly say the list is incomplete rather than reconstructing it from rumours.
