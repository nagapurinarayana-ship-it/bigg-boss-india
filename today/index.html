Latest verified multi-language developments

Hindi 20: September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the four nominees. Fresh Episode 4 reporting highlights a Mary Kom–Rhiti Tiwari kitchen-duty clash and an Arishfa–Aamrapali dispute over an age-related comment. Telugu 10: Moneycontrol reports that 10 contestants received special “magical powers” from launch tasks, including reported nomination, safety and captaincy advantages. No newer verified competition result was found in this scan, so the existing tracker is preserved.

Tamil 10: Shathiga is the first captain. September 9 coverage reports a heated task-rule discussion involving Shathiga, Muniyamma and Jason. Indian Express Tamil now reports that Black Pandi has left the house because of continuing health concerns and the need for medical assistance, updating the earlier promo-only possibility.

Kannada 13: September 9 Cinema Express coverage reports that Gagan Chinnappa's Diamond-task strategy put Aasiya Firdose in the spotlight; the report says Aasiya was left in tears after the result. New Kannada Filmibeat coverage also reports a heated Kiran Shastry–Vaishnavi Kowdinya exchange over bathroom-use rules shown in a Colors Kannada promo. These are reported house/task developments, not official disciplinary decisions.

Malayalam 8: Gayathri Suresh was evicted on Day 2 after the Democracy/Janasabha twist. Fresh September 9 reporting confirms Jaseela Parveen as the first leader and Times of India reports Rahul Easwar has entered the house. New Indian Express Malayalam reporting says Rini Ann George broke down during a verbal clash with Rahul Easwar and described severe personal and career distress; those are reported contestant statements, not independently established claims. Another current report says RJ Anjali discussed the cyber-attacks she says she experienced and their impact; this is also treated as a reported contestant account.

Bangla 2026: Week 2 nominees remain Jayita Chatterjee, Srijla Guha, Dipendu Biswas, Bharat Kaul, Durnibar Saha and Alexandra Taylor. New Indian Express Bengali reporting says wildcard Rajveer Dey called housemates a “herd of sheep” during a task, prompting Dipendu Biswas to confront him. A newer report also describes a Rajveer–Tanushree verbal clash during the Bagh-Bandi task; Rajveer's reported promise to make Jayita captain and any eventual captaincy result remain unconfirmed.
