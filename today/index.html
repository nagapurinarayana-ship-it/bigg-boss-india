September 10, 2026

Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam launched September 6. Bangla launched August 30 and is also underway.

Latest verified multi-language developments

Hindi 20: September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the four nominees, with Gullu, Aasif Khan and Yung DSA reported as captaincy contenders. A fresh JioHotstar Reality promo, reported by Free Press Journal, shows Qazi Touqeer in heated exchanges with Aasif Khan, Kanika Mann and Aamrapali Dubey during the captaincy task; the promo suggests an intense confrontation but does not establish that a physical fight occurred. Some later entertainment reports claim Yung DSA won the captaincy, but contemporaneous coverage conflicts, so the winner is not treated as confirmed. Episode 4 coverage also includes the Mary Kom–Rhiti Tiwari kitchen-duty clash and Arishfa–Aamrapali dispute. A current Times of India report says singer Arijit Singh will watch because of Qazi Touqeer; this is celebrity reaction, not a game result.

Telugu 10: Moneycontrol reports that 10 contestants received special “magical powers” affecting nominations, safety and captaincy. Its September 9 coverage also highlights a Balu–Meena house-tour promo featuring Vishnukant and Amulya Gowda. The promo is entertainment coverage, not a contestant-entry or elimination result.

Tamil 10: Shathiga is the first captain. September 9 reporting covers the Shathiga–Muniyamma–Jason task dispute, while Indian Express Tamil reports that Black Pandi has left the house because of continuing health concerns and the need for medical assistance.

Kannada 13: Cinema Express reports Gagan Chinnappa's Diamond-task strategy put Aasiya Firdose in the spotlight and left her emotional after the result. Current coverage also reports a Kiran Shastry–Vaishnavi Kowdinya bathroom-rule clash. The first reported nominees are Manju, Avinash, Likhith, Dhanush, Kiran Shastry, Vaishnavi, Soundarya Shetty and Prathama Prasad.

Malayalam 8: Gayathri Suresh was evicted on Day 2, Jaseela Parveen is the first leader and Rahul Easwar has entered. Times of India now reports the first eviction round has nine nominees: Kadal Komban, Eldho, Neha Fathima, Muhammed Azif, Noby, Rahul Easwar, Kaali, Shijin and Rini Ann George. The report says Rini received the most nomination votes; Jaseela, Mridhula Vijai and Sumesh Chandran were saved as leader/supporters. New Indian Express Malayalam coverage also reports a heated Rini–Rahul exchange in which Rini described severe mental pressure; these are reported contestant statements, not independently established facts outside the show.

Bangla 2026: Week 2 nominees remain Jayita Chatterjee, Srijla Guha, Dipendu Biswas, Bharat Kaul, Durnibar Saha and Alexandra Taylor. Indian Express Bengali reports Rajveer Dey's “herd of sheep” clash with Dipendu and a newer Rajveer–Tanushree dispute during the Bagh-Bandi task, with Tanushree challenging Rajveer's strategy and leadership. Rajveer's reported promise to make Jayita captain remains unconfirmed; the eventual captaincy outcome depends on the task.
