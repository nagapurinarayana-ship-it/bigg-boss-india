September 10, 2026

Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam launched September 6. Bangla launched August 30 and is also underway.

Latest verified multi-language developments

Hindi 20: September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the four nominees, with Gullu, Aasif Khan and Yung DSA reported as captaincy contenders. A fresh JioHotstar Reality promo, reported by Free Press Journal, shows Qazi Touqeer in heated exchanges with Aasif Khan, Kanika Mann and Aamrapali Dubey during the captaincy task; the promo suggests an intense confrontation but does not establish that a physical fight occurred. Some later entertainment reports claim Yung DSA won the captaincy, but contemporaneous coverage conflicts, so the winner is not treated as confirmed. A newer Jansatta report also describes Yung DSA accidentally bringing up rapper Badshah while speaking with Isha Rikhi, then apologising after realising the context; this is reported episode coverage, not a conclusion about their private lives. Episode 4 coverage also includes the Mary Kom–Rhiti Tiwari kitchen-duty clash and Arishfa–Aamrapali dispute.

Telugu 10: Moneycontrol reports that 10 contestants received special “magical powers” affecting nominations, safety and captaincy. Its September 9 coverage also highlights a Balu–Meena house-tour promo featuring Vishnukant and Amulya Gowda. The promo is entertainment coverage, not a contestant-entry or elimination result.

Tamil 10: Shathiga is the first captain. September 9 reporting covers the Shathiga–Muniyamma–Jason task dispute, while Indian Express Tamil reports that Black Pandi has left the house because of continuing health concerns and the need for medical assistance. Fresh September 9 coverage also reports that Avinash Ashok entered after receiving more audience votes than Akshadha Ashok in the premiere's new selection process; this is a premiere result rather than a current elimination.

Kannada 13: Cinema Express reports Gagan Chinnappa's Diamond-task strategy put Aasiya Firdose in the spotlight and left her emotional after the result. Current coverage also reports a Kiran Shastry–Vaishnavi Kowdinya bathroom-rule clash. The first reported nominees are Manju, Avinash, Likhith, Dhanush, Kiran Shastry, Vaishnavi, Soundarya Shetty and Prathama Prasad. New September 10 reporting adds the Madiddunnno Maraya food task: Asianet Newsable reports the flight crew won the food task and that Manja and Gagan Chinnappa then exchanged words. This is reported task/promo coverage, not an official disciplinary finding.

Malayalam 8: Gayathri Suresh was evicted on Day 2, Jaseela Parveen is the first leader and Rahul Easwar has entered. Times of India reports the first eviction round has nine nominees: Kadal Komban, Eldho, Neha Fathima, Muhammed Azif, Noby, Rahul Easwar, Kaali, Shijin and Rini Ann George. Indian Express Malayalam reports a heated Rini–Rahul exchange in which Rini described severe mental pressure. It also reports that Bigg Boss Malayalam 5 winner Akhil Marar criticised Jaseela's leadership after the Day 2 eviction. Fresh September 10 reporting adds Gayathri Suresh's own reaction: Indian Express Malayalam reports that she said she initially thought the eviction was a prank, called the decision unfair and said she believed housemates had voted to evict Rini before the captain's decision changed the outcome. These are reported statements from Gayathri, not independent findings.

Bangla 2026: Week 2 nominees remain Jayita Chatterjee, Srijla Guha, Dipendu Biswas, Bharat Kaul, Durnibar Saha and Alexandra Taylor. Indian Express Bengali reports Rajveer Dey's “herd of sheep” clash with Dipendu and the newer Rajveer–Tanushree dispute during the Bagh-Bandi task. Fresh September 10 reporting adds a Rajveer–Shayak Chakraborty confrontation: Indian Express Bengali reports that Rajveer used the phrase “chamgi mumgi” while taunting Shayak during a task and Shayak responded angrily. The same report says Jayita separately accused Shayak of physical harassment and misbehaviour during a task; this is presented only as a reported allegation, not an established fact. Rajveer's reported promise to make Jayita captain remains unconfirmed.
