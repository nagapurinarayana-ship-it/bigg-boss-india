🆕 Fresh Bigg Boss news — September 10

Hindi 20: September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the first four nominees, with Gullu, Aasif Khan and Yung DSA reported as captaincy contenders. A fresh JioHotstar Reality promo, reported by Free Press Journal, shows Qazi Touqeer in heated exchanges with Aasif Khan, Kanika Mann and Aamrapali Dubey during the captaincy task; it suggests an intense confrontation but does not establish that a physical fight occurred. Some later entertainment reports claim Yung DSA won the captaincy, but contemporaneous reports conflict, so the winner is not treated as confirmed. A newer Jansatta report also describes Yung DSA accidentally bringing up rapper Badshah while speaking with Isha Rikhi, then apologising after realising the context; this remains reported episode coverage. Telugu 10: Moneycontrol reports 10 contestants received secret “magical powers” affecting nominations, safety or captaincy, and its latest September 9 coverage highlights a Balu–Meena house-tour promo featuring Vishnukant and Amulya Gowda. This is promotional coverage, not a contestant-entry or elimination result. Tamil 10: Shathiga is the first captain, with task-related disputes reported; Indian Express reports Black Pandi has left the house after continuing health concerns and the need for medical assistance. Fresh September 9 coverage also reports that Avinash Ashok entered after receiving more audience votes than Akshadha Ashok in the premiere's new selection process; this is a premiere result rather than a current elimination. Kannada 13: September 9 reporting highlights Gagan Chinnappa's Diamond-task strategy, Aasiya Firdose fallout and a reported Kiran Shastry–Vaishnavi Kowdinya bathroom-rule clash. Cinema Express also lists the first reported nominees as Manju, Avinash, Likhith, Dhanush, Kiran Shastry, Vaishnavi, Soundarya Shetty and Prathama Prasad. New September 10 reporting adds the Madiddunnno Maraya food task, with the flight crew reported to have won and Manja and Gagan Chinnappa exchanging words. Malayalam 8: Gayathri Suresh was evicted on Day 2, Jaseela Parveen is the first leader and Rahul Easwar has entered. New September 9 reporting says the first eviction round has nine nominees: Kadal Komban, Eldho, Neha Fathima, Muhammed Azif, Noby, Rahul Easwar, Kaali, Shijin and Rini Ann George. Newer Indian Express Malayalam reporting covers the Rini–Rahul exchange and Rini's claims of severe mental pressure, while another report says Akhil Marar criticised Jaseela's leadership. Fresh September 10 preview coverage from Times of India reports a Sreelakshmi Arakkal–Rini Ann George food dispute in the kitchen. Latest September 10 reporting adds a democracy-themed format twist: Times of India reports that the house leader and two supporters are protected from eviction while the leader can decide that one contestant leaves; host Mohanlal is reported to have described the setup as democratic leadership rather than autocracy. This is current media reporting about the format and is not presented as a permanent rule beyond the reported episode context. Bangla: Week 2 nominations are reported as Jayita, Srijla, Dipendu, Bharat, Durnibar and Alexandra. Current Indian Express Bengali coverage reports Rajveer Dey's clashes with Dipendu, Tanushree and Shayak; Jayita's separate allegation about Shayak remains explicitly an allegation. These are reported developments unless explicitly identified as official.
