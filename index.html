🔥 Bigg Boss 2026: five editions premiere September 6

The biggest Bigg Boss weekend of 2026 is now officially confirmed. JioStar says Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20, Tamil 10, Telugu 10, Kannada 13 and Malayalam 8 all premiere on Sunday, September 6, 2026. Bigg Boss Bangla launched on August 30 and is already underway.

Official update today — September 4

JioStar's September 4 announcement confirms six Bigg Boss editions across Hindi, Bangla, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam, with more than 95 sponsors across the franchise. It names Salman Khan for Hindi, Sourav Ganguly for Bangla, Vijay Sethupathi for Tamil, Nagarjuna for Telugu, Kiccha Sudeep for Kannada and Mohanlal for Malayalam. Read the official JioStar release →

🔥 Bigg Boss 20 premiere time — latest update

Bigg Boss 20 premieres September 6. Current published entertainment reports list the grand premiere at 9:00 PM on JioHotstar and 10:30 PM on Colors TV. The September 6 date itself is officially confirmed by JioStar; the viewing times should be checked against the latest platform and TV listings on premiere day.
