Editorial rule: appearing in a pre-show is not the same as entering the Bigg Boss house. We keep appeared, selected, on hold, not selected and house contestant as separate statuses and leave unknown results unfilled instead of guessing.

How we track every contestant

  1. Appearance: confirm that the person actually appeared in the selection/pre-show.
  2. Round & task: record the exact challenge or stage when a reliable report establishes it.
  3. Decision: mark selected, on hold or not selected only when supported.
  4. Next round: follow the participant forward instead of treating every appearance as house entry.
  5. Main-house status: only mark a contestant as a housemate after a verified main-show entry.

Quick answers

Are all names on this site confirmed Bigg Boss house contestants?

No. A person may be an audition participant, selected participant, on hold or simply featured in a promo. We label those states separately.

Why don't you publish rumours?

Because a fast but wrong contestant list is less useful than a smaller verified tracker. Rumours are not converted into confirmed names.

How often is the site updated?

The homepage is dated. The date is changed when the content genuinely changes, not simply to make an old page look fresh.

Where should I start for contestant results?

Open the edition card above. Each hub is designed around its own current search intent and contains the known names, process, status and next step.
