Bigg Boss Malayalam 8: Gayathri Suresh evicted on Day 2

Updated: September 9, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 8 launched on September 6, 2026 with Mohanlal as host. In the latest major development, Gayathri Suresh was evicted on Day 2, an unusually early exit. Current reports say the Democracy/Janasabha process gave captain Jaseela Parveen and her assistants a decisive role after the house discussion.

Day 2: first eviction twist

Times of India reports that housemates were offered a choice involving a restricted food arrangement for the season or eliminating a contestant. The house discussion initially produced majority support for the food option, while the captain and assistants selected the eviction route. The captain then sought a house choice and ultimately decided that Gayathri Suresh would leave.

Indian Express likewise reports the 48-hour spot-eviction twist and Jaseela Parveen's decision.

Fresh verification: September 9 reporting continues to describe this as the season's historic Day-2 exit. No official comeback confirmation was found in the current sources reviewed.
