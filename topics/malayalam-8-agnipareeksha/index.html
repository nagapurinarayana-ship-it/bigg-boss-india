Bigg Boss Malayalam 8: Jaseela leads, Rahul Easwar enters amid Rini clash

Updated: September 9, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 8 launched on September 6, 2026 with Mohanlal as host. Current September 9 reporting confirms Jaseela Parveen as the first leader and reports Rahul Easwar's entry into the house. The opening phase also produced the historic Day 2 eviction of Gayathri Suresh through the Democracy/Janasabha twist.

September 9: new house conflict

Times of India reports that Jaseela Parveen is the first leader of the house, while separate current reporting confirms Rahul Easwar's entry.

Fresh Rini-Rahul clash: Indian Express Malayalam reports that contestant Rini Ann George broke down during a verbal argument with Rahul Easwar, saying she felt her career and life had been damaged and that she was under severe mental pressure. These are Rini's statements reported from the episode; the page does not present them as independently established facts about events outside the house.

Earlier leadership commentary: Indian Express Malayalam also reported former winner Akhil Marar's criticism of Jaseela Parveen's leadership. That criticism remains commentary from a former participant, not an official programme ruling.

Day 2 history: Times of India and Indian Express reporting continue to describe Gayathri Suresh's exit as the season's historic Day-2 eviction through the Democracy/Janasabha mechanism. No official comeback confirmation was found in the current sources reviewed.
