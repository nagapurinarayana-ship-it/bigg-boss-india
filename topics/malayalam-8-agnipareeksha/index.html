Bigg Boss Malayalam 8: Jaseela leads, Rahul Easwar enters as Gayathri exits on Day 2

Updated: September 9, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 8 launched on September 6, 2026 with Mohanlal as host. Current September 9 reporting confirms Jaseela Parveen as the first leader and reports Rahul Easwar's entry into the house. The opening phase also produced the historic Day 2 eviction of Gayathri Suresh through the Democracy/Janasabha twist.

Day 2: first eviction twist and leadership debate

Times of India reports that housemates were offered a choice involving a restricted food arrangement for the season or eliminating a contestant. The house discussion initially produced majority support for the food option, while the captain and assistants selected the eviction route. The captain then sought a house choice and ultimately decided that Gayathri Suresh would leave.

Indian Express likewise reports the 48-hour spot-eviction twist and Jaseela Parveen's decision.

First leader confirmed: separate September 9 Times of India reporting identifies Jaseela Parveen as the first leader of the house, with Mridula and Sumesh also receiving notable advantages during the opening week.

Fresh house-entry report: Times of India reports that Rahul Easwar entered the Bigg Boss Malayalam 8 house. This is a current contestant-entry report; it is not presented as an eviction, wildcard outcome or official comeback claim for any other contestant.

Fresh reaction: Indian Express Malayalam reports that former winner and commentator Akhil Marar criticised Jaseela Parveen's leadership, arguing that the Democracy theme was not reflected in the way the decision was handled and questioning whether the captain acted more like a ruler than a leader. This is commentary from a former participant, not an official programme ruling.

Fresh verification: September 9 reporting continues to describe Gayathri's exit as the season's historic Day-2 eviction. No official comeback confirmation was found in the current sources reviewed.
