Bigg Boss Malayalam 8: nine nominees, Gayathri reaction and latest house clash

Updated: September 10, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 8 launched on September 6, 2026 with Mohanlal as host. Current September 9 reporting says nine contestants are facing the first eviction round, while Jaseela Parveen is the first leader and Rahul Easwar has entered the house. The opening phase also produced the historic Day 2 eviction of Gayathri Suresh through the Democracy/Janasabha twist.

September 9: first eviction list

Times of India reports that the first open nomination produced nine contestants facing eviction: Kadal Komban, Eldho, Neha Fathima, Muhammed Azif, Noby, Rahul Easwar, Kaali, Shijin and Rini Ann George. The report says Rini received the maximum number of nomination votes. Jaseela Parveen, Mridhula Vijai and Sumesh Chandran were saved as the leader and her supporters.

The same report says contestants were allowed to discuss why they should be saved before the open nomination. Eldho nominated Rini and Shijin, Anjali questioned Rini's repeated references to outside issues, Rahul accused Rini of lacking game spirit, and Rini nominated Rahul after he mentioned a “fake case”. These are reported episode exchanges, not independent findings about events outside the show.

September 10: fresh reaction and house developments

Times of India reports that Jaseela Parveen is the first leader of the house and that Rahul Easwar has entered the house. The page preserves these as current media reports pending a stronger official broadcaster announcement.

Fresh Gayathri reaction: Indian Express Malayalam reports that Gayathri Suresh posted a video reacting to her Day-2 eviction. She said she initially thought the eviction might be a prank, described the decision as unfair, and said she had expected more time to show her game. She also said that, according to her account, housemates had voted to evict Rini before the captain's decision changed the outcome. These are Gayathri's reported statements about her experience, not an independent finding.

Fresh Rini-Rahul clash: Indian Express Malayalam reports that Rini Ann George broke down during a verbal argument with Rahul Easwar, saying she felt her career and life had been damaged and that she was under severe mental pressure. These are Rini's statements reported from the episode; the page does not present them as independently established facts about events outside the house.

Fresh food dispute: Times of India reports a new preview in which Sreelakshmi Arakkal and Rini Ann George have a heated altercation over food in the kitchen. The report describes this as an upcoming episode development and says food-related disagreements had already begun involving the house leader and other participants. This is preview/reporting coverage, not an official disciplinary finding.

Fresh reaction to Jaseela's leadership: Indian Express Malayalam reports that Bigg Boss Malayalam Season 5 winner Akhil Marar criticised Jaseela Parveen's leadership after the Day 2 eviction and questioned whether she was acting as a leader or ruling like a king. The report says his criticism focused on Jaseela's decision to evict Gayathri and the wider Democracy/Janasabha process. This is Akhil Marar's reported opinion, not an official judgement by the show.

RJ Anjali discussion: Indian Express Malayalam reports that RJ Anjali spoke with Vicky about the cyber-attacks she says she experienced and their impact on her and her family. This remains a reported contestant account.

Day 2 history

Gayathri Suresh became the first eviction on Day 2 under the Democracy/Janasabha mechanism. The historical result is preserved rather than replaced by the newer nomination round.
