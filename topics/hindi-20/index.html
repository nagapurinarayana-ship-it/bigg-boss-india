Bigg Boss Hindi 20: Episode 4 captaincy, nominations and drama

Updated: September 10, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20 launched on September 6, 2026 with Salman Khan. Current September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the first four nominees.

Episode 4: latest house developments

The first nomination exercise ended with Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill nominated for the week. The captaincy task had Gullu, Aasif Khan and Yung DSA as the reported contenders after the 2X-room lifeline sequence.

Captaincy result: Jansatta's September 9 Episode 4 report says Yung DSA was declared the first captain of Bigg Boss Hindi 20 after the captaincy task. This is now treated as the latest reported result rather than leaving the winner unresolved; the site still identifies it as media reporting unless an official broadcaster announcement is available.

New captaincy-task promo coverage: A September 9 promo shared by JioHotstar Reality and reported by Free Press Journal shows Qazi Touqeer in heated exchanges with Aasif Khan, Kanika Mann and Aamrapali Dubey during the captaincy task. The promo suggests an intense confrontation, but it does not by itself establish that a physical fight occurred.

Fresh Episode 4 reporting: Navbharat Times reports a heated kitchen-duty exchange between MC Mary Kom and Rhiti Tiwari. The report also covers Qazi Touqeer's comic sequence and renewed house tension around chores and captaincy. These are reported episode/promo developments, not official disciplinary findings.

Arishfa–Aamrapali dispute: Jansatta reports that Arishfa Khan and Aamrapali Dubey argued after an age-related comment during the episode. Mahhi Vij also became involved before Arishfa later apologised. This is reported episode coverage, not an official finding about either contestant.

Newer house conversation: Jansatta reports that Yung DSA accidentally brought up rapper Badshah while speaking with Isha Rikhi, before realising the connection and apologising. The incident is reported as an episode conversation and viral clip; the site does not infer anything beyond what the report describes.

Relationship discussion: India Today reports that when Yung referred to being a “Badshah”, Isha avoided naming the musician directly and replied “ex” and “No comments” when questioned. The site records this as reported episode dialogue, not as an independently verified relationship claim.

Eviction timing: Navbharat Times reports that there will be no Weekend Ka Vaar eviction this week. Because this is media reporting rather than a direct broadcaster schedule notice, it remains labelled as reported and may change.
