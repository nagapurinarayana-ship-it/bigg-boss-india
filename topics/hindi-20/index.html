Bigg Boss Hindi 20: Episode 3 nominations, captaincy and drama

Updated: September 9, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20 launched on September 6, 2026 with Salman Khan. Current September 9 reporting confirms Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill as the first four nominees.

Episode 3: nominations and captaincy race

Times of India reports that the first nomination exercise ended with Kanika Mann, Uditi Singh, Arishfa Khan and Love Gill nominated for the week. The captaincy task currently has Gullu, Aasif Khan and Yung DSA as the reported contenders after the 2X-room lifeline sequence.

Fresh episode drama: September 9 reporting describes a confrontation between Arishfa Khan and Aamrapali Dubey after Arishfa objected to being treated as a child. Mahhi Vij also joined the argument, and Arishfa later apologised to Aamrapali.

Latest promo: Navbharat Times reports another upcoming house clash involving MC Mary Kom and Rhiti Tiwari over kitchen duties, while Qazi Touqeer provides comic drama. The same report says the captaincy task remains a current focus and indicates there will be no Weekend Ka Vaar eviction this week; this is reported promo information, not an official schedule guarantee.
