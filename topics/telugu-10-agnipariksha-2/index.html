Bigg Boss Telugu 10: Maha Pareeksha high-risk zone and latest updates

Updated: September 10, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Telugu Season 10 launched on September 6, 2026 with Nagarjuna as host. September 10 Telugu360 reporting says the Maha Pareeksha continued with both Red and Blue teams found to have violated task rules. Neither team was declared the winner, and Bigg Boss reportedly imposed a major punishment by sending one member from each team into a high-risk zone.

September 10: reported Maha Pareeksha high-risk zone

According to Telugu360's current episode coverage, the Blue team selected Ram Prasad, who volunteered for the high-risk zone. The other team selected Charan after a more difficult discussion. This is current media reporting of the task and punishment; it is not presented as an official eviction or audience-vote result.

Earlier: reported magical-power twist

According to current reporting, contestants who won power keys during the launch received different advantages. Reported examples include Srishti having the ability to nominate someone directly, comedian Ram Prasad having a Safe Shield, Vamsi being able to block a contestant from a captaincy race for a period, and Jabardasth Naresh having a double-vote power.

These details are explicitly labelled as media reporting rather than an official JioStar announcement. No fan-poll percentages are used as evidence of official voting.

The promo highlights early drama and mind games. It is promotional entertainment coverage, not a contestant-entry, nomination or elimination result.
