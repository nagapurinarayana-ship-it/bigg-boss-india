The Common Man: Latest buzz

Updated: August 16, 2026 · Latest verified report

The short answer: The Common Man is the new pre-show route for Bigg Boss Tamil 10, giving ordinary participants a chance to compete for a place in the main house.

What's happening?

The format puts aspiring contestants through physical, mental and emotional challenges before the main-season selection. The show is scheduled as a 21-episode pre-launch series, with episodes on Vijay TV and JioHotstar.

Today's buzz

A recent promo featuring Muniyamma and judge Raju Jeyamohan has attracted attention for its lively exchange and gives viewers an early look at the personalities entering the selection process.

Who are the judges?

The judging panel includes Oviya, Aari Arujunan and Raju Jeyamohan. Maya Sundarakrishnan hosts the pre-show.

Status: The Common Man selection series is the current Tamil Bigg Boss story.
