Bigg Boss Kannada 13: first nominations are in

Updated: September 9, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Kannada Season 13 launched on September 6, 2026 with Kichcha Sudeepa as host. Current Kannada reporting confirms the first nomination round and an additional danger-zone development involving Aasiya Firdose.

September 9: first nomination round

Vishwavani reports that the first nominees are Manju, Avinash, Likhith, Dhanush, Kiran Shastry, Vaishnavi, Soundarya Shetty and Prathama Prasad. The report says contestants in the flight area were nominated, while some could still become safe.

The same current coverage reports that Gagan Chinnappa won the Kohinoor challenge and became a VIP, while Aasiya Firdose lost that challenge and returned to the flight area, placing her in the danger zone.

September 9: Gagan–Aasiya task fallout

Cinema Express reports that Gagan Chinnappa's strategy during the Diamond task put Aasiya Firdose in the spotlight. A contestant who identified the diamond holder could gain nomination safety and VIP status; Aasiya questioned Gagan's targeting strategy, and the task ended with several contestants suspecting Aasiya had the diamond. The report says she was left in tears after the result. These details are presented as reported episode developments.

Other September 9 Kannada coverage also reports a heated Gagan Chinnappa–Manju exchange and a food task that split contestants into teams and tested how quickly they could finish unpleasant food. These are reported task developments, not fan-poll results.
