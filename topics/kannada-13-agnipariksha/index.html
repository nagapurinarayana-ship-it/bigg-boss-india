Bigg Boss Kannada 13: food-task clash, first nominations and Day 2 drama

Updated: September 11, 2026 · Live-season tracker

Bigg Boss Kannada Season 13 launched on September 6, 2026 with Kichcha Sudeepa as host. Current reporting tracks the first nomination round, Aasiya Firdose's danger-zone storyline and the latest food-task drama involving Gagan Chinnappa and Manja.

First-week nominations

Current reporting lists Manju, Avinash, Likhith, Dhanush, Kiran Shastry, Vaishnavi, Soundarya Shetty and Prathama Prasad among the first reported nominees. Aasiya Firdose has also featured in the early danger-zone storyline after the Kohinoor challenge.

Latest task developments

Asianet Newsable reports that the Madiddunnno Maraya food task involved the flight crew and housemates, with the flight team reported to have won and Manja and Gagan Chinnappa exchanging words afterwards. This is reported task/promo coverage, not an official disciplinary finding.
