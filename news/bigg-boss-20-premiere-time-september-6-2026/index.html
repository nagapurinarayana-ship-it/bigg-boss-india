Bigg Boss 20 premiere time: September 6 on JioHotstar and Colors TV

Updated: September 4, 2026 · Verified date, current published viewing reports

Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20 premieres on Sunday, September 6, 2026, with Salman Khan as host. JioStar's September 4 official franchise announcement confirms the September 6 launch. Current published entertainment reports list the digital premiere on JioHotstar at 9 PM and the television broadcast on Colors TV at 10:30 PM.
