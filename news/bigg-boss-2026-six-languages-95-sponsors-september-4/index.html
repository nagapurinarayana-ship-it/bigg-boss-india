Bigg Boss 2026: JioStar confirms six languages and 95+ sponsors

Updated: September 4, 2026 · Primary source: JioStar official newsroom

JioStar has confirmed a major 2026 expansion of the Bigg Boss franchise, with six language editions and more than 95 marquee sponsors across the festive-season run.

Five editions premiere on September 6

JioStar confirms that Bigg Boss Hindi Season 20, Tamil 10, Telugu 10, Kannada 13 and Malayalam 8 premiere on Sunday, September 6, 2026. Bigg Boss Bangla launched on August 30, so it is already live.

Who hosts each Bigg Boss edition?

  • Hindi 20: Salman Khan
  • Bangla: Sourav Ganguly
  • Tamil 10: Vijay Sethupathi
  • Telugu 10: Nagarjuna
  • Kannada 13: Kiccha Sudeep
  • Malayalam 8: Mohanlal

Why September 6 matters

The simultaneous South launch creates one of the biggest Bigg Boss weekends of the year. Fans searching for premiere dates, hosts, streaming information, contestants and voting details will have five editions starting on the same Sunday, while Bangla is already producing episodes.
