Bigg Boss 20 house tour 2026: rooms, pool, Room 20 and 104 cameras

Updated: September 5, 2026 · Premiere-eve house reveal

The Bigg Boss 20 house has been revealed ahead of the September 6 premiere. Current coverage describes a fantasy-driven design inspired by Alice in Wonderland, with unusual rooms, a shark-themed pool, a hot-air-balloon seating area and a mysterious new Room 20. The house is reported to cover about 10,000 square feet and use 104 cameras.

Bigg Boss 20 house: what is inside?

  • Design: fantasy/duality concept with Alice in Wonderland-inspired elements.
  • Size: about 10,000 square feet, according to current house-tour coverage.
  • Cameras: 104 cameras are reported across the house.
  • Garden: large animal installations, including a gorilla and a snake-covered tree.
  • Pool: a distinctive shark-themed swimming pool.
  • Kitchen: designed around a vintage food-truck concept.
  • Living room: central seating, parachute-style seating and a pink teapot throne.
  • Bedroom: mushroom-style artwork, eight beds and oversized beds among the fantasy décor.

What is Room 20?

Room 20 is one of the biggest mysteries in the new house. Current house-reveal coverage identifies it as a new addition, but its exact role and the rules attached to it have not been fully explained by the makers in the material reviewed for this page.

That distinction matters: the room exists as part of the revealed house, but its future function should not be presented as a confirmed twist until Bigg Boss officially explains it.

Reports also point to additional mystery/secret spaces designed to create surprise during the season. Their exact access rules, occupants and tasks remain subject to official Bigg Boss announcements. We will update this page when the show confirms how these spaces work.

Why the Bigg Boss 20 house is different

The new house leans heavily into fantasy, contrast and visual puzzles rather than a conventional residential look. The combination of oversized installations, themed furniture, unusual seating and hidden-room speculation gives the premiere a strong visual hook.

The house is also designed for continuous observation. Current reports put the camera count at 104, reinforcing how closely contestants' activities will be monitored once the season starts.
