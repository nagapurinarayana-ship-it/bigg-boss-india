Bigg Boss Tamil 10: Manivannan enters as first wild card

Updated: September 11, 2026 · Source-led update

Bigg Boss Tamil 10 is getting its first new entrant. Times of India reports that Common Man contestant Manivannan has entered as the first wild-card contestant, adding a fresh dynamic to the early season.

What the wild-card entry means

Manivannan joins the existing housemates after the season's opening days. The entry is reported as a Common Man participation route and is expected to change early alliances and task dynamics.
