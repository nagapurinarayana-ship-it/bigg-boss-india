Bigg Boss 2026 premiere date & schedule: all 6 languages

JioStar's latest official announcement confirms a six-edition Bigg Boss 2026 franchise. Bigg Boss Hindi 20, Telugu 10, Tamil 10, Kannada 13 and Malayalam 8 premiere on September 6, 2026. Bigg Boss Bangla premiered on August 30 and is already underway.

Bigg Boss 2026 schedule

  • Hindi 20: September 6 — Salman Khan
  • Telugu 10: September 6 — Nagarjuna
  • Tamil 10: September 6 — Vijay Sethupathi
  • Kannada 13: September 6 — Kiccha Sudeep
  • Malayalam 8: September 6 — Mohanlal
  • Bangla 2026: August 30 — Sourav Ganguly

Why September 6 matters

The 2026 season brings five editions to a coordinated September 6 launch, while Bangla started earlier. JioStar says the franchise spans six languages and has more than 95 sponsors.
